The Mission

Every life contains wisdom
no one has collected yet.

The world is getting better at storing information. It is not getting better at preserving what experienced humans actually know — the judgment, the pattern recognition, the instincts built through decades of real-world practice.

EdTerm was built to solve that problem. We conduct structured interviews with experienced professionals and transform their knowledge into Wisdom Nodes — structured, searchable artifacts that transfer what was previously untransferable.

The methodology is called Wisdom Journalism. It passed double-blind peer review through the International Academic Forum and is being presented at the Asian Conference on Education in Tokyo, November 2026.

Human Experience

"One lesson took me nearly twenty years to trust. Sometimes the patient who looks the calmest is the one I'm most worried about. When a normally anxious patient suddenly becomes unusually quiet, avoids eye contact, and their family starts answering questions for them — I stop assuming they're improving. I've learned to slow down, reassess, and trust that subtle change. More than once, those patients deteriorated within the hour."

Decision Framework

Has the patient's behavior changed unexpectedly? Is their family behaving differently? Does my clinical intuition conflict with the vital signs? If several indicators are present — escalate. Even if standard metrics have not crossed intervention thresholds.

Transferable Wisdom

Experienced clinicians don't ignore the data — they recognize when the data hasn't caught up to reality.
