03 / 09

The Solution

Structured interviews.
Permanent, searchable wisdom.

EdTerm conducts structured interviews with retiring professionals using Wisdom Journalism — a peer-reviewed methodology — and transforms their judgment into Wisdom Nodes: six-component structured knowledge artifacts.

Our AI layer, Tero, surfaces the right node to the right person at the right moment. A second-year ICU nurse describes what she is seeing. Tero matches her to the nodes of nurses who navigated the same threshold.

Node #004831 Senior Emergency Department Nurse · 32 Years Clinical Experience

Threshold Moment

"Sometimes the patient who looks the calmest is the one I'm most worried about. When a normally anxious patient suddenly becomes unusually quiet and their family starts answering questions for them — I stop. More than once those patients deteriorated within the hour."

Decision Framework

Has behavior changed unexpectedly? Is the family behaving differently? Does clinical intuition conflict with the vitals? If several indicators present — escalate. Even if metrics haven't crossed intervention thresholds.

Transferable Insight

Experienced clinicians don't ignore the data — they recognize when the data hasn't caught up to reality.
