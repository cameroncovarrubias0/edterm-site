EdTerm for Universities

The loop has always been broken. Here’s how we close it.

You send graduates into the world prepared. What they become over a career — the judgment, the instincts, the hard decisions made under pressure — never finds its way back. Not because practitioners don’t want to share it. Because there was never a structure to capture it. EdTerm is that structure.

The Problem

What gets lost

Every field loses its most valuable knowledge the same way. A veteran practitioner retires. A senior clinician moves on. A decade of expertise walks out the door as institutional memory, not curriculum. What they knew was never documented — not because it wasn’t real, but because no methodology existed to capture it at that level of depth and structure.

The Solution

What comes back

Wisdom Journalism is a structured interview methodology that surfaces the tacit knowledge experienced practitioners carry — the decisions, the patterns, the judgment calls that define expertise. Each interview produces a Wisdom Node: a structured, searchable record of professional knowledge that can feed directly into curriculum development, training programs, and institutional memory.

This is not a podcast. It is not a lecture series. It is infrastructure.

The Full Circle

University → Career → Data → Curriculum

Universities prepare people for industry, but the knowledge those people develop over a career never comes back. The loop is broken. Wisdom Journalism is how we close it — structured capture of expert professional judgment that feeds back into curriculum, so the next generation trains on what practitioners actually learned, not just what was documented when they left school.
