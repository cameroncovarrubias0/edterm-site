EdTerm for Universities

Two crises.
One solution.

Journalism is losing its institutional home at the same moment that human wisdom is disappearing undocumented. Wisdom Journalism is the bridge between them.

"The skills journalism programs have always taught are precisely the skills required to collect human experience at scale. This is not a retreat from relevance — it is a redefinition of it."

Double-Blind Peer Review Passed ACE2026 · Tokyo · November 2026 Pilot Study In Development

The Argument

Two simultaneous crises —
one structural solution.

Crisis One

Journalism is losing its professional infrastructure.

Newsrooms have shed over a third of their workforce since 2008. Journalism schools are training students for roles that no longer exist at scale. The field needs a new professional purpose — and a new argument for why the skills it teaches matter.

Wisdom Journalism

Crisis Two

Human wisdom is disappearing undocumented.

Every day, experienced professionals retire without documentation of what they actually knew — how they actually decided, what took decades to develop and cannot be replaced by a manual, a training module, or an AI model trained on generic text.

What Wisdom Journalism Is

A new professional purpose
for skills that already exist.

Students are trained not to produce news content but to collect, structure, and preserve threshold professional experiences as Wisdom Nodes — discrete, transferable knowledge units designed to enter the Human Wisdom Database and become accessible to anyone who needs them. The curriculum framework is peer-reviewed. The pilot that validates it in practice is what we are building next.

What students do

Collect human experience at scale

Trained in structured interviewing, narrative construction, and elicitation methodology — the skills journalism has always taught, applied to a new purpose. Each student produces real Wisdom Nodes that enter the Human Wisdom Database.

What the work produces

Permanent, searchable knowledge

Wisdom Nodes — six-component structured artifacts that capture how experienced professionals actually think and decide under pressure. Not summaries. Not testimonials. Structured knowledge that transfers what was previously untransferable.

Why it matters in the AI era

The work that cannot be automated

In a world transformed by AI, collecting lived human experience requires precisely the human skills AI cannot replicate — presence, trust, the ability to elicit what someone experienced rather than what they prepared to say.

The curriculum framework

Peer-reviewed methodology, pilot in development

The Wisdom Journalism methodology passed double-blind peer review — the framework has academic standing. The next step is real-world validation through a structured pilot. That is what we are building now, and it is what partner universities help create.

The Global Mission

Wisdom is culturally constructed.
The database must reflect that.

What counts as a threshold moment in one culture is shaped by history, economic context, and social structure in ways completely different from another. A database that collects only one culture's wisdom will fail the people who most need it.

EdTerm is building a global network of university research partners — not implementation partners but cultural co-architects. Each regional partner helps define what wisdom transfer looks like in their specific context. Each partner contributes nodes that no other institution could produce.

"Together they build a database that represents humanity rather than a subset of it."

We are actively seeking first partners across every continent. The methodology for collecting wisdom from a specific region can only be developed by someone who knows that region from the inside.

Cultural co-architects

Partner universities don't implement a finished product. They help shape what the methodology becomes in their specific cultural and academic context.

Original research contribution

Every regional partner produces Wisdom Nodes that could not exist without their specific cultural knowledge. This is not replication — it is original contribution to a global archive.

International academic recognition

Co-authorship on peer-reviewed publications. Participation in international conference presentations. Named membership in a global research network building something that has never been built before.

A database built to last

The Human Wisdom Database is designed to outlast any individual institution. Partner universities are not customers — they are founding contributors to a permanent global archive of lived human experience.
